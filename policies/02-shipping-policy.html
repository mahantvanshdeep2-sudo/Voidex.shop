Shipping Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Processing time

Orders are picked and dispatched within 1 business day of purchase. Orders placed on a weekend or a Canadian public holiday are dispatched the next business day.

Delivery times

These are the same timeframes shown on the shipping options at checkout. If the two ever disagree, the figure shown at checkout is the one that applies to your order.

Shipping cost

Shipping is free on every order, to every destination we serve. There are no handling fees and no minimum order value.

Tracking

You receive a confirmation email with a tracking number as soon as your order is dispatched. If it has not arrived within 24 hours of your dispatch email, check your spam folder and then contact us.

Customs and import duties

Orders delivered outside Canada may be subject to import duties, taxes or customs fees charged by the destination country. These are set by that country, are not collected by us, and are the responsibility of the recipient.

Delays

Occasionally a shipment is delayed by customs, a carrier backlog or unusually high demand. We will tell you if your order is materially delayed. If it has not arrived within 30 days of purchase, you are entitled to a full refund under our Refund Policy.

Incorrect addresses

Please check your delivery address carefully at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us within 12 hours of ordering and we will correct it before dispatch. We cannot redirect a parcel once it has shipped.
