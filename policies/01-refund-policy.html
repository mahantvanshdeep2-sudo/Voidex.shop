Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

If you are not fully satisfied with your order from VOIDEX.SHOP, you may request a return or refund within 30 days of the delivery date.

The 30-day promise

If the seam splits, the zip fails, or the product simply is not what you expected, email us within 30 days and we will refund you in full. For the Sneaker Wash Bag you do not need to ship it back — keep it.

If your order never arrives

If your order has not arrived within 30 days of purchase, we refund you in full, on request. You do not have to chase us for it.

Eligibility for a standard return

  • Items must be unused, unworn and in their original packaging.
  • Your order number is required as proof of purchase.

How to request a return

Email mahantvanshdeep2@gmail.com with your order number and the reason for the return. We respond within 2 business days.

Refund processing

Once a refund is approved, it is issued to your original payment method. Your bank or card issuer typically takes a further 5–10 business days to post it to your account.

Return shipping costs

Where a physical return is required, you cover return shipping — unless the item arrived damaged, defective or incorrect, in which case we cover it.

Damaged or incorrect items

Contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the problem and we will arrange a replacement or a full refund at no cost to you.
